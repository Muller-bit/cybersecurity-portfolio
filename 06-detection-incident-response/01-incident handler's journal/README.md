## 🔍 The 5 W's Analysis

### 1. Who caused the incident?

An organized group of unethical hackers known for targeting organizations in the healthcare and transportation industries caused the incident. They executed the attack by sending targeted phishing emails to several employees.

### 2. What happened?

A severe ransomware attack occurred where malicious attachments in phishing emails installed malware on employee computers, subsequently encrypting critical files and medical records. This caused a complete shutdown of business operations and a ransom note demanding money for the decryption key was displayed.

### 3. When did the incident occur?

The security incident took place on a Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 a.m., immediately halting normal operations at the clinic.

### 4. Where did the incident happen?

The incident happened at a small U.S. health care clinic specializing in primary-care services, affecting workstations and network systems across the organization.

### 5. Why did the incident happen?

The incident happened because attackers successfully exploited human vulnerability via targeted phishing emails containing malicious attachments, allowing unauthorized access and deployment of ransomware across unprotected endpoints.

## 💡 Additional Notes & Open Questions

- **Initial Analytical Thoughts:**
  This incident highlights how critical human factors are in cybersecurity; a single successful phishing email can completely paralyze a healthcare facility. Immediate priorities must include isolating affected systems to prevent lateral movement and engaging external incident response or legal partners.

- **Key Investigation Questions:**
  1. What specific strain or variant of ransomware was deployed?
  2. Are there immutable offline backups available to restore operations without paying the ransom?
  3. Which specific email accounts were compromised, and what initial foothold did the attackers use post-phishing?
